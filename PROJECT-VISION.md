# NOVA — One AI Platform, Two Personal Experiences

NOVA is an AI companion platform with two distinct experiences:

1. **NOVA Kids:** A friendly, patient learning companion that helps children talk about their day, understand school subjects, practise homework, explore everyday knowledge and develop new skills.

2. **NOVA Personal:** A memory-inspired AI companion for adults. Users can create a personality profile from information and memories they are authorized to use. NOVA can use that profile to generate personalized conversations, with a visual avatar or looping video experience as part of the long-term vision.

## The main motive

NOVA began with one question:

**What if AI could become more personal, useful and connected to the experiences that matter in everyday life?**

For children, NOVA aims to make learning more conversational, enjoyable and accessible.

For adults, NOVA aims to help preserve meaningful stories, memories and personality-inspired experiences through AI.

## NOVA Kids — Learning companion

The intended experience includes:

- After-school conversations about the child's day.
- Homework explanations and guided problem-solving.
- Age-appropriate general knowledge.
- Daily learning activities and revision.
- Practice in languages, mathematics, science, drawing, coding and other skills.
- Personal learning preferences and progress.
- Patient, encouraging conversations.

Child safety is a core requirement: parent-managed controls, age-appropriate safeguards, privacy protection, separate child memory and appropriate limits. NOVA is intended to support parents and educators, not replace them.

## NOVA Personal — Memory-inspired companion

The intended experience allows adults to create a profile inspired by someone meaningful to them.

A profile may contain:

- Name and relationship.
- Personality and communication style.
- Stories, memories and life experiences.
- Favourite expressions, interests and values.
- Authorized photos, recordings or other media.

NOVA can use this supplied context to generate conversations inspired by the profile.

The long-term visual experience includes an on-screen avatar or looping video, with future possibilities such as animated expressions and lip synchronization.

**Transparency matters:** This is an AI-generated simulation, not the actual person or their consciousness. Likeness, recordings and voice should only be used with appropriate rights and consent. NOVA must not encourage users to confuse an AI simulation with a real person.

## Shared technology

Both experiences can use common platform foundations:

- Configurable AI providers.
- Text and voice interaction.
- Conversation history.
- Memory and profile management.
- Personalization.
- Avatar interface.
- User-controlled settings and privacy.

The two modes must keep their private data and memories separate.

## Future roadmap

1. Improve the Android companion foundation.
2. Build child learning journeys and parental controls.
3. Develop guided personality and memory profile creation.
4. Improve natural voice conversations.
5. Develop animated avatars and visual companion experiences.
6. Improve long-term memory, export, deletion and optional encrypted synchronization.
7. Complete security, child-safety and production-release testing.

## Current implementation status

NOVA 1.4.0 is a preview build. Existing foundations include provider settings, local conversation history, editable memory notes, structured person profiles, voice interaction components and an approval workflow for supported actions.

The full Kids learning experience, comprehensive parental controls, advanced personality simulation, animated avatar with lip synchronization and advanced memory capabilities are development goals, not claims of completed functionality.

## The mission

**One AI platform. Two generations. Two deeply personal experiences.**

NOVA aims to make AI more human-centered: helping children learn and grow, while helping adults preserve and revisit meaningful memories through transparent, user-controlled AI experiences.

**NOVA — Learn together. Remember meaningfully. Grow with AI.**
