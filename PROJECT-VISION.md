# NOVA — One AI Platform, Two Personal Experiences

## The vision

NOVA is an AI companion platform designed around two important human needs: helping children learn, grow and feel supported, and helping adults preserve meaningful connections through personalized AI experiences.

The central idea is simple:

**AI should not only answer questions. It should understand the context of a person's life, communicate in a way that feels personal, and help make everyday experiences more meaningful.**

NOVA brings these ideas together in one Android-based platform with two separate companion modes: NOVA Kids and NOVA Personal.

---

## 1. NOVA Kids — A friendly learning and growth companion

NOVA Kids is designed to be a friendly, patient and supportive AI learning companion for children.

Imagine a child coming home from school. Instead of only using a phone for entertainment, the child can open NOVA and talk about their day.

The child might say:

- "Today I learned about the solar system."
- "I didn't understand this maths problem."
- "Can you help me with my homework?"
- "Teach me something interesting."
- "I want to learn drawing, English or coding."
- "Can we practise what I learned today?"

NOVA Kids should respond in a child-friendly way, understand the child's learning level and encourage curiosity.

### What NOVA Kids aims to provide

**A. After-school conversations**

- Let children talk about their school day.
- Ask what they learned and what they found difficult.
- Encourage children to express their thoughts.
- Help them reflect on their daily experiences.

**B. Homework and learning support**

- Explain difficult topics in simple language.
- Break problems into manageable steps.
- Give hints and examples instead of simply giving every answer.
- Help children revise lessons and prepare for tests.
- Adapt explanations to the child's age and learning level.

**C. Everyday knowledge**

- Answer age-appropriate questions.
- Explain science, nature, technology, history and the world.
- Encourage curiosity and critical thinking.
- Teach useful everyday knowledge.

**D. New skills and hobbies**

- Introduce drawing, writing, languages, mathematics, coding and other skills.
- Create small daily learning activities.
- Encourage practice and celebrate progress.
- Help children discover their interests.

**E. Personal learning memory**

- Remember the child's learning preferences and goals.
- Track topics they have practised.
- Identify areas where they need more explanation.
- Offer revision based on previous learning.

**F. A friendly personality**

- Be patient, encouraging and respectful.
- Explain things without making children feel embarrassed.
- Make learning conversational and enjoyable.
- Encourage real-world activities, family interaction and healthy routines.

### Child safety is essential

NOVA Kids must be designed with parental supervision and age-appropriate safeguards.

The intended product should include:

- Parent-managed setup and controls.
- Separate child and adult memory.
- Age-appropriate responses and content restrictions.
- Privacy-conscious data collection.
- Appropriate screen-time and usage controls.
- Clear escalation to a parent or trusted adult when a child needs help.
- No manipulative emotional dependency or pressure to keep chatting.

NOVA is intended to support parents and educators, not replace them.

## 2. NOVA Personal — A personalized, memory-inspired AI companion

NOVA Personal is designed around a different human need: preserving meaningful memories and creating a personal conversational experience inspired by someone important.

A person may have lost someone they love, live far away from someone, or simply want to preserve the stories, personality and communication style of a person who has played an important role in their life.

The user can create a personal profile by providing information such as:

- The person's name and relationship to the user.
- Personality and character.
- How the person speaks and communicates.
- Favourite expressions and conversational habits.
- Stories, memories and life experiences.
- Values, interests and preferences.
- Photos, recordings or other materials they are authorized to use.

NOVA can use this information to create a personalized AI character that responds in a style inspired by the supplied profile and memories.

### What NOVA Personal aims to provide

**A. Personality creation**

The user can build a detailed personality profile describing how the person behaves, communicates, thinks and responds.

**B. Memory-based conversations**

NOVA can use supplied stories and memories as conversational context, helping the user revisit meaningful experiences and discuss remembered events.

**C. Personalized communication**

The AI can be configured around a person's communication style, vocabulary, tone, humour and conversational preferences.

**D. Visual companion experience**

The long-term vision includes an on-screen avatar or character that remains visible during conversations.

Possible experiences include:

- A looping video or animated character.
- A personalized visual appearance.
- A conversational screen layout.
- Future real-time facial animation and lip synchronization.
- Voice interaction where legally and ethically authorized.

The intention is to make conversations feel more personal and visually engaging, rather than interacting only with a text box.

**E. User-controlled profiles**

Users should be able to edit, pause, export or delete a personality profile and its associated memories.

### An important distinction

NOVA Personal is an AI-generated simulation inspired by user-provided memories and information. It is not the actual person, their consciousness, or a way to communicate with someone who has died.

The experience must be transparent about its AI-generated nature. Voice, likeness and recordings should only be used with appropriate rights and consent. NOVA must not encourage users to mistake an AI simulation for a real person or replace real human relationships and professional support.

## 3. One platform, two independent experiences

Both modes can share the underlying NOVA technology while keeping their experiences, data and safeguards separate.

Shared platform foundations:

- AI provider selection.
- Conversation engine.
- Voice interaction.
- Memory and profile management.
- Avatar and visual interface.
- Personalization.
- Privacy and security controls.

Separate experiences:

- NOVA Kids: learning, development, child safety and parent controls.
- NOVA Personal: personality profiles, memory-inspired conversations and user-controlled visual companions.

The two modes must not accidentally share private memories or personal data.

---

## 4. Why NOVA is different

NOVA is being developed around the idea that AI should be personal, contextual and useful.

It combines two directions that are often treated separately:

1. AI as a learning companion that helps the next generation explore and develop skills.
2. AI as a memory-inspired personal companion that helps adults preserve and revisit meaningful stories and personalities.

The project aims to bring both experiences into one accessible Android application.

## 5. Long-term product roadmap

### Stage 1 — Core Android companion

- AI provider configuration.
- Text and voice interaction foundations.
- Local conversation history.
- Editable notes and structured profiles.
- Basic action approval workflow.
- Separate adult and kids modes.

### Stage 2 — NOVA Kids development

- Parent onboarding and controls.
- Age and learning-level configuration.
- Homework explanation and guided practice.
- Daily learning activities.
- Learning progress and revision memory.
- Strong child-safety testing.

### Stage 3 — NOVA Personal development

- Guided personality-profile creation.
- Structured life stories and memory management.
- Context-aware personality-inspired conversations.
- User-controlled avatar profiles.
- Authorized media and voice support.
- Clear AI identity and transparency safeguards.

### Stage 4 — Visual and voice experience

- Improved real-time voice conversations.
- Animated companion interface.
- Looping video or avatar playback.
- Future lip-sync and facial animation.
- Better conversational responsiveness.

### Stage 5 — Secure personal AI platform

- Better long-term memory retrieval.
- Optional encrypted backup and synchronization.
- Strong privacy and deletion controls.
- Improved device integrations.
- Production-grade Android release and testing.

These stages describe the intended development direction. They are not claims that every feature is currently implemented.

---

## 6. The main motive

NOVA began with a question:

**What if an AI companion could become more personal, more useful and more connected to the experiences that matter in everyday life?**

For a child, that could mean having a patient learning companion who makes education more engaging.

For an adult, it could mean having a personalized AI experience that preserves stories, memories and the communication style of someone meaningful.

The broader ambition is to make AI more human-centered while keeping people, their choices and their safety at the center.

**NOVA — Learn together. Remember meaningfully. Grow with AI.**

---

## Current project status

NOVA 1.4.0 is an Android preview build. The existing application contains foundations for provider configuration, local conversation history, editable memory, structured profiles, voice interaction and approval-based actions.

The complete two-experience vision described above is the product direction. Advanced child-learning features, comprehensive parental controls, full visual avatar animation, lip synchronization, advanced memory intelligence and other roadmap items require further implementation and testing.

NOVA is an independent project and is not an official Google or DeepSeek product.
